## Overview
Today we'll be focusing on reflecting on strategies for interdisciplinary collaboration and preparing for the final project.

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:
* Identify difficulties and strategies for interdisciplinary collaboration
* Run your AEco and/or DEvo projects and discuss the code with peers

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:
* Make improvements to your projects based on feedback
* Create a basic artificial life simulation based on an ODE

## Checks
Submit answers to the following on Moodle:
* What aspects of collaborating with the ODE students did you find most difficult?
* What strategies did you use to translate from your artificial ecology to the information needed to construct an ODE?
