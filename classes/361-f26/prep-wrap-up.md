## Overview
What a term it has been! To wrap things up, we're going to remind ourselves of the goals of the field of artificial life and think about how you can carry forth the lessons you've learned, no matter your path.

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:
* Explain Beer's critiques of the field of ALife
* Explain Harvey's rebuttal

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:
* Define for yourself what you have found most valuable to take forward from this class and field


## Reading
I know it's the end of term and you are busy, so I get it if you need to just skim, but I promise you that these are some fun and spicy articles that really are worth printing out and reading. 

Read the following:
* [(A)Life as It Could Be by Randall D. Beer](https://direct.mit.edu/artl/article/30/4/539/124845/A-Life-as-It-Could-Be)
* [Comment on Randall D. Beer’s “A(Life) as It Could Be” by Inman Harvey](https://direct.mit.edu/artl/article/30/4/546/124846/Comment-on-Randall-D-Beer-s-A-Life-as-It-Could-Be)
