## Overview
You've now learned about both ecological dynamics and evolutionary dynamics. However, previous systems have isolated those two forces, which isn't how biological evolution actually works. When you combine ecological and evolutionary dynamics together, things get complicated fast, but that's where artificial life can be the most exciting! Today we'll discuss my particular research system that is focused on the co-evolutionary dynamics of symbiosis specifically: Symbulation.

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:
* Explain how symbionts interact with hosts in the system
* Identify two examples of the evolutionary effects symbionts can have on their hosts

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:
* Expand on Symbulation to conduct a novel investigation
