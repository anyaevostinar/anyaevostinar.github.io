## Overview
L-systems are another example of a simple idea in theory that can be leveraged for large amounts of emergent complexity. Today we'll be focusing on a specific example use of them in a system called Geb.

## AI Policy
As a reminder, you are not allowed to use genAI to summarize or produce your reading check responses. These checks are for you to engage with the material and start thinking about things before class. It's okay if you don't understand everything ahead of time!

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:

* Explain the high-level goals and idea of Geb
* Explain how L-systems are used in Geb

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:

* Explain the specifics of how Geb works

## Reading
Read the following:

* [Evolving Novel Behaviors via Natural Selection](/classes/361-f26/Evolving_Novel_Behaviors_via_Natural_Sel.pdf) by Channon and Damper

## Checks
Submit answers to the following on Moodle:
* What scientific questions/goals was Geb created to address?
* What dynamics of life are L-systems capturing in Geb and how do they do it?
