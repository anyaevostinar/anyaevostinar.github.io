## Overview
Today's class will be focused on code review of your final project ABMs to get help on any bugs and make improvements.


## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:

* Explain the idea of your final project paper
* Discuss where you are at with your final project ABM

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:

* Make improvements to your projects based on feedback


## Checks
Submit answers to the following on Moodle:
* What have you completed on your final project?
* What are your next steps for the final project?
* What is one thing that you want to show off regarding your final project?
* What is one thing you'd like help on for your final project?
