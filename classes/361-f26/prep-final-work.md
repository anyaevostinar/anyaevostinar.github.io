## Overview
Today's class will be focused on making significant progress on the basic functionality of your final project's ABM.

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:
* Explain the species and interactions involved in the ODE
* Explain your plan for implementing those interactions in an ABM

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:
* Complete you ABM implementation
* Collect data to compare to the ODE

## Checks
Submit answers to the following on Moodle:
* What are the species that you will need to implement?
* How do those species impact each other and how will you implement that interaction?
* What data do you need to output to compare to the ODE?
