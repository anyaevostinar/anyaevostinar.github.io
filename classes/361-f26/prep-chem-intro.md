## Overview
While the origins of life are still being investigated, there must have been a transition from free-floating chemicals to some kind of contained cell structure. The subfield of artificial chemistry is focused on this pre-cell time and the transition to cells. Today, we'll learn a little bit about how researchers in this field use simulations to study these general dynamics.
