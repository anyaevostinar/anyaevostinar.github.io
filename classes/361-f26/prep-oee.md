## Overview
Our final new topic of the term is the biggest one: how is it that nature is continuously producing such novel and interesting creatures?? This idea is term "open-ended evolution" and is considered one of the "grand challenges" of artificial life. Today, we'll discuss how I and colleagues have proposed tackling it!

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:
* Define open-ended evolution in the context of nature and artificial life broadly
* Explain the high-level ideas behind the MODES metrics

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:
* Explain the approach of the MODES metrics more in depth

## Resources

* [The MODES Toolbox: Measurements of Open-Ended Dynamics in Evolving Systems](https://direct.mit.edu/artl/article/25/1/50/2915/The-MODES-Toolbox-Measurements-of-Open-Ended)
