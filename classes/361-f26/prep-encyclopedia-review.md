## Overview
Class will be focused on getting feedback on your encyclopedia drafts. Therefore, most of your focus for prep should be on any further updates that you want to make to your draft before class, however there are some reflective questions to submit as well. You are certainly welcome to continue to update your encyclopedia draft after submitting as well.

## AI Policy
As a reminder, you are not allowed to use genAI to summarize or produce your reading check responses. These checks are for you to engage with the material and start thinking about things before class. It's okay if you don't understand everything ahead of time!

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:

* Discuss your encyclopedia first draft with your peers

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:

* Improve your draft based on feedback

## Checks
Be sure to submit your draft to the form linked on Moodle for Wednesday in addition to submitting an answer to the following:
* Where are your at with your draft and what have you found the most challenging aspect of writing this piece? What have you found the most interesting/rewarding?
