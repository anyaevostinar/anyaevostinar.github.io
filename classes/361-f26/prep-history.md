## Overview
Humanity has been recreating the dynamics of life in artificial forms since before we were really humans: cave paintings, life-like sculptures, clockwork automata, and more. Today, we'll do a very quick tour of this history as an introduction to the broad areas and mindset of the field.

## AI Policy
As a reminder, you are not allowed to use genAI to summarize or produce your reading check responses. These checks are for you to engage with the material and start thinking about things before class. It's okay if you don't understand everything ahead of time!

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:
* List and explain the generally agreed upon dynamics/requirements for life
* Explain the high-level idea of autopoiesis

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:
* Identify historical examples of artificial life systems
* Explain how the artificial life mindset has appeared throughout history
* Connect historical artificial life examples to modern innovations
