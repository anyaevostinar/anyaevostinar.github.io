## Overview
A particular type of growth that embodies the emergence of complexity from simple rules is that of seemingly-complex patterns, such as on animal fur and skin. It's also a fun area that a pioneer of computer science, Alan Turing, weighed in on!

## AI Policy
As a reminder, you are not allowed to use genAI to summarize or produce your reading check responses. These checks are for you to engage with the material and start thinking about things before class. It's okay if you don't understand everything ahead of time!

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:

* Explain the high-level ideas of reaction-diffusion systems
* Identify some of the ways that reaction-diffusion systems can capture dynamics of life

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:

* Use a reaction-diffusion system in an artificial life model for a specific goal
