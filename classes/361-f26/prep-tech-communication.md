## Overview
Today's class will be focused on skills of technical communication focused on your final project presentations.

## Basic Learning Objectives
Before class, you should be able to:
* Explain the high-level ideas of your assigned paper
* Explain how your ABM captures those high-level ideas 

## Advanced Learning Objectives
After class, you should be able to:
* Create a presentation explaining your ABM and results


## Reading
As a start to wrapping up the course, I'd like you to read a few "meta" articles from senior researchers in artificial life. 
The first is a recent editorial on the use of genAI/LLMs to produce papers, which I hope you find both entertaining and inspiring in your battle against the seduction of allowing LLMs to complete your presentation for you

* [Artificial Life and How to Avoid AI Psychosis](https://direct.mit.edu/artl/article/32/3/253/139564/Artificial-Life-and-How-to-Avoid-AI-Psychosis)

## Checks
Create a Google Slide show, change viewing permissions to Carleton, and share the link on Moodle. Your slide show should have the following three slides:
* An outline of your paper's main ideas
* An outline of your ABM's implementation of those ideas (text or a custom made figure if you are feeling inspired!)
* A summary of your ABM's main results

In addition, submit your favorite quote from the avoiding AI Psychosis article (there are many fantastic ones to choose from!).
